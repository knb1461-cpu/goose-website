ABOUT GOOSE HUB

What Is Goose Hub?

Goose Hub is a student-created website made for NMS students.

The idea is simple: bring games, updates, and entertainment together in one place.

No complicated menus. No boring design. Just Goose Hub.

THE ORIGIN STORY

Why I Made This Website

Let's face it. School can get boring sometimes.

Especially when you have free time and you're tired of playing the exact same games.

That's why I created Goose Hub.

I wanted to make a place where NMS students could discover games, explore something different, and have a little fun during their free time.

Instead of looking through random websites, I wanted to put everything together in one organized place.

Goose Hub is also my way of learning how to code, build websites, and turn an idea into something real.

Created by the Real Goose Kristoff. For NMS only.
